# Your figure: options

Sixteen candidate busts for the human seat, in the style of the opponents' figures. The cards are teal, your name color on the board.

- `options.png` shows each option at full size (160 × 160) and at the 30 px seat size.
- `01.svg` to `16.svg` are the drawings.
- `gen.py` builds the drawings from interchangeable parts: hair, headwear, glasses, beard, clothing and expression.

To rebuild the drawings, run `python3 gen.py <folder>`.
