Joker suit pip

Proposal for the flipped-Joker house rule. The center star and JOKER text stay. When the Joker carries a suit, that suit sits up and to the left of the center star, like a card index. The corner star is unchanged. No stroke. With the default re-deal rule, the Joker is unchanged.
