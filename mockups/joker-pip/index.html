Joker suit pip

Proposal for the flipped-Joker house rule. The center star and JOKER text stay. When the Joker carries a suit, that suit sits directly under the corner star, like a card index. All four suits are drawn the same width. No stroke. With the default re-deal rule, the Joker is unchanged.
