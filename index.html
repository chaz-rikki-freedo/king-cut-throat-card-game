      King Cut-Throat

      A three-player trick-taking card game. Every player for themselves; first to 10 points wins.

      Original game design by C.R.F.B. 2009–2011

      Build dev

      A new version is ready.

      Settings

      Theme

      FeltThe color of the table: the classic green, or a season.

      Table memory

      What every player has seen this hand, added to the popups. Never a card someone holds unseen.

      House rules

      Redeal: the hand is void. A suit: the Joker offers that suit for trump, to save a deal. Applies from your next game.

      Your seat

      Who the AI plays as in your seat, and how: Settings → Bots → Your seat. A game it plays clears no wave.

      Who sits in each seat, and how each bot plays. Its own keeps a bot's personality; any other choice changes that one thing. Changes apply from your next game. A bot you change does not count in your results.

      Waves and Dynamic always seat their own bots, playing as themselves. West and East apply in Free play only. Your seat applies in every mode.

      DefaultsEvery seat: the seed picks who sits, and each bot plays as itself.
